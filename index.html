How the library works

1. Teachers Upload

Staff sign in with the teacher account, upload a file and describe it — title, subject, year group and more.

2. Reviewed & Approved

New submissions wait in a review queue. Once an administrator approves them they appear in the library instantly.

3. Search & Use

Students search by subject, topic or year group, preview resources in the browser and download them for offline use.
